    Questions, answered

    Will it survive a drop?

    The coir fibers in the barrel work like the straw in an adobe brick. They hold the clay together so it resists cracking on a bag-to-floor fall. Hard drops on concrete can still chip the barrel, and the newspaper and kraft wrap keep a chip from becoming a break.

    Can the lead handle heat?

    Beeswax softens in high heat, but the coconut charcoal and clay powder give the lead its backbone. It handles a warm classroom or a bag in a jeepney. Avoid leaving it on a dashboard in direct sun.

    Is anything in it wood?

    No. The barrel uses clay, coir, wood ash, and gawgaw (starch). The wrap is recycled diaryo, thin carton, and kraft tape. The leads use coconut charcoal, beeswax, clay, and coconut oil.

    Which seeds work in the cap?

    A size 00 capsule fits small seeds such as pechay, basil, chili, tomato, and marigold. Use the tracker below to see what to expect from each.

    What happens to the cap after planting?

    The tissue barrier and clay plug soften with moisture, so the seed can sprout while the capsule breaks down in the soil.
